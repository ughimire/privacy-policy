## Privacy Policy

### The short version

Life Quotes has **no accounts, no login, and no server of ours**. Your quotes,
your favourites, anything you write, your streak, your reminder times and any
photo you choose as a card background all stay **on your device**. We cannot see
them, because they are never sent anywhere.

The one exception is advertising, which is how the app stays free. That is
described in full below.

---

### 1. What stays on your device

All of the following is stored locally, in the app's private storage, and is
never transmitted to us or to anyone else:

- The quote library and which categories you have chosen
- Quotes you save, collections, and any quotes you write yourself
- Card designs you create in the Studio
- Your reading history (used only to avoid repeating quotes)
- Your daily streak and reminder times
- Photos you pick as a card background — the app copies the image you choose
  into its own storage so the card keeps working, and that copy never leaves
  your device

**Deleting the app deletes all of it.** There is nothing held elsewhere to
request, export or erase, because nothing was ever collected.

---

### 2. Advertising

Life Quotes shows ads supplied by **Google AdMob**. Ads are what pay for the
app; there is no subscription and no purchase.

Google, acting as an independent advertising provider, may collect and process
data when an ad is requested or shown, including:

- Advertising identifiers (Android Advertising ID, iOS IDFA where permitted)
- IP address and approximate, non-precise location derived from it
- Device and app information, such as model, operating system and app version
- Interactions with ads, such as views and taps

We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you. We see only
aggregate reports — impressions, clicks, revenue.

Google's handling of this data is governed by:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
- [Google Advertising Policies](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543)

**Your choices**

- **In the EEA, UK and Switzerland** the app shows a consent form, provided by
  Google's User Messaging Platform, before ads are personalised. You may accept
  or refuse; refusing still shows ads, just non-personalised ones. You can
  change your choice at any time from within the app.
- **On iOS**, personalised advertising additionally requires your permission
  through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. Declining is respected.
- **On Android**, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in
  *Settings → Google → Ads*.
- **On iOS**, you can disable tracking in *Settings → Privacy & Security →
  Tracking*.

**Rewarded ads.** Some optional extras — additional themes, additional
typefaces, removing the wordmark from an exported image — are unlocked by
watching a short ad. This is always your choice, always described before the ad
plays, and nothing is unlocked unless the ad actually finishes.

### 3. Analytics

**We use no analytics SDK.** There is no Firebase Analytics, no crash
reporting, and no third-party measurement in this app. We do not track how you
use it.

If that changes, this policy will be updated before the change ships, and the
"last updated" date above will move.

---

### 4. Permissions

| Permission | Why | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Notifications** | To deliver the daily quote reminders you set up | Requested only when you enable a reminder. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device — no server is involved. |
| **Photos** | To let you use one of your own photos as a card background | Uses the system photo picker. The app never gains access to your photo library — only to the single image you select. |
| **Save to gallery** | To save a card you designed | Only writes the image you asked to save. |
| **Internet** | To load ads | The quote library works fully offline. |
| **Run at startup** | To re-arm your reminders after the device restarts | Otherwise reminders would silently stop after a reboot. |

Life Quotes does **not** request access to your contacts, microphone, camera,
precise location, files, or your photo library as a whole.

---

### 5. Children

Life Quotes is not directed at children and is not intended for use by children
under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly
collect any information from children. Ads are configured for a general
audience.

---

### 6. Data retention and deletion

Because nothing is collected on a server, there is nothing for us to retain.
Everything the app stores lives on your device and is removed when you uninstall
the app, or when you clear its storage from your device settings.

For data held by Google in connection with advertising, please refer to Google's
own privacy policy and controls linked in section 2.

---

### 7. Security

App data is held in the app's private storage area, which the operating system
isolates from other apps. Since no personal data is transmitted, there is no
transmission for anyone to intercept.
