## Privacy Policy

This describes what the app actually does, checked against its source code
rather than against intentions.

### The short version

- **Roof Tape collects nothing.** There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics,
  no advertising and no tracking of any kind.
- **Your jobs stay on your device.** Measurements, prices, photos, your business
  profile and every quote you write are stored locally.
- **The app makes no network requests of its own.** It contains no networking
  code at all. The only servers involved are Apple's, and only for two things
  you choose: buying Pro, and iCloud Sync if you switch it on.
- **It works completely offline**, which is the point — roofs are measured in
  places with no signal.

### 1. What the app stores, and where

Roof Tape stores the following on your device:

| Data | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Jobs | Customer name, phone, email, site address, job status |
| Roof measurements | Sections, shapes, pitches, lengths, edge lengths |
| Materials and pricing | Your price book, waste percentages, markup, tax, deposit |
| Photos | Site photos you attach to a job, and their captions |
| Business profile | Business name, contact details, licence number, insurance note, logo |
| Quotes | Quote numbers, notes, terms, and the customer's signature if they sign |

All of it lives in a database on your device. None of it is sent to MantraBrain,
and we have no servers that could receive it.

### 2. iCloud Sync

iCloud Sync is **off unless you turn it on** in Settings.

When it is on, the same data is stored in **your own private iCloud database**,
under your Apple Account, in the container `iCloud.com.mantrabrain.rooftape`.
This is Apple's CloudKit private database: it is yours, it counts against your
iCloud storage, and MantraBrain cannot read it, list it or recover it. If you
turn Sync off, the app goes back to using only the copy on your device.

Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by the
[Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

### 3. Photos

When you add a photo to a job, the app opens the **system photo picker**. That
picker runs outside the app, so Roof Tape never gets access to your photo
library — it only receives the specific images you pick. Those images are stored
with the job and are included on the quote PDF you generate.

### 4. Motion sensors

The **Pitch Finder** screen reads the phone's tilt sensors so you can hold the
phone against a rafter and read the pitch. The reading is shown on screen and
used in your calculation. It is not recorded, stored or transmitted, and the
sensors are not read anywhere else in the app.

### 5. Purchases

Roof Tape Pro is sold through Apple as an annual subscription or a one-time
lifetime purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your name,
card, address or Apple Account. The app only ever learns whether an entitlement
is active.

### 6. Sharing is under your control

When you share a quote, a materials list or a backup file, it goes wherever you
send it — Messages, Mail, Files, AirDrop, a printer. That destination's own
privacy terms apply. Nothing is shared automatically.

### 7. Children

Roof Tape is a tool for roofing contractors. It is not directed at children
under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

### 8. Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct
or delete on request. To remove everything the app holds, delete the app; to
also remove the iCloud copy, turn off iCloud Sync or delete the app's data in
iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.
