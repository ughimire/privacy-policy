## Privacy Policy

This describes what the app actually does, checked against its source code
rather than against intentions.

### The short version

- **Fuel Note collects nothing.** There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics,
  no advertising and no tracking of any kind.
- **Your vehicle history stays on your device.** Fill-ups, services, expenses,
  reminders and receipt photos are stored locally.
- **The app makes no network requests of its own.** It contains no networking
  code at all. The only servers involved are Apple's, and only for two things
  you choose: buying Pro, and iCloud Sync if you switch it on.
- **Reminders are scheduled on your phone**, not by a server. Fuel Note sends no
  push notifications and has no push capability.

### 1. What the app stores, and where

Fuel Note stores the following on your device:

| Data | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Vehicles | Name, make, model, year, VIN, licence plate, units, currency |
| Fill-ups | Date, odometer, fuel amount, cost, station, fuel grade, notes |
| Services | Date, odometer, type, cost, workshop, notes |
| Expenses | Date, category, amount, notes |
| Reminders | Service type, interval by time or distance, due dates |
| Receipts | Photos you attach to a fill-up or service |

Your VIN and licence plate are stored only because a buyer expects to see them
on a service history report. They are never transmitted anywhere.

All of it lives in a database on your device. None of it is sent to MantraBrain,
and we have no servers that could receive it.

### 2. iCloud Sync

iCloud Sync is **off unless you turn it on** in Settings.

When it is on, the same data is stored in **your own private iCloud database**,
under your Apple Account, in the container `iCloud.com.mantrabrain.fuelnote`.
This is Apple's CloudKit private database: it is yours, it counts against your
iCloud storage, and MantraBrain cannot read it, list it or recover it. If you
turn Sync off, the app goes back to using only the copy on your device.

Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by the
[Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

### 3. The Home Screen widget

The widget cannot read the app's database directly, so the app writes a small
summary — vehicle name, latest fuel economy, next service due, this month's
spend, last odometer reading — into a shared container on your device that only
Fuel Note and its widget can open. It stays on your device and is rewritten
whenever your data changes.

### 4. Photos

When you attach a receipt or a vehicle photo, the app opens the **system photo
picker**. That picker runs outside the app, so Fuel Note never gets access to
your photo library — it only receives the specific images you pick. Receipt
photos are stored with the record and can be included in the service history
PDF you generate.

### 5. Notifications

Service reminders are scheduled **locally on your device** by iOS. Nothing is
sent to or from a server, and the app has no push notification capability. You
can turn notifications off at any time in iOS Settings.

### 6. Importing and exporting

Fuel Note can import a CSV exported from Fuelly, aCar, Fuelio, Drivvo or a
spreadsheet. The file is read on your device and never uploaded. Exports — CSV
files and the PDF service history report — are written on your device and go
only where you choose to send them.

### 7. Purchases

Fuel Note Pro is sold through Apple as a monthly or annual subscription, or a
one-time lifetime purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Apple. We never see
your name, card, address or Apple Account. The app only ever learns whether an
entitlement is active.

### 8. Sharing is under your control

When you share a report, a CSV or a backup file, it goes wherever you send it —
Messages, Mail, Files, AirDrop, a printer. That destination's own privacy terms
apply. Nothing is shared automatically.

### 9. Children

Fuel Note is a tool for vehicle owners. It is not directed at children under 13
and collects no data from anyone.

### 10. Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct
or delete on request. To remove everything the app holds, delete the app; to
also remove the iCloud copy, turn off iCloud Sync or delete the app's data in
iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

### What the app is, and is not

Fuel Note is a **record-keeping and estimating aid**. Fuel economy figures, cost
projections and predicted service dates are calculated from the entries you make
and are only as accurate as those entries.

**Reminders are a convenience, not a guarantee.** Do not rely on the app alone
for safety-critical maintenance. A missed or partial fill-up, a backdated entry
or an odometer typo will shift the calculations, and iOS may delay or withhold a
local notification. Always follow your manufacturer's service schedule.

A service history report produced by the app is a summary of what you entered.
It is not a certified vehicle history, an inspection, or a warranty, and it does
not verify that any work was carried out. To the extent permitted by law,
MantraBrain is not liable for missed maintenance, vehicle damage, disputes over
a sale, or any loss arising from reliance on the app's output.
