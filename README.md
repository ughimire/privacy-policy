
# Privacy Policy for Chrome Extensions by Umesh Ghimire

**Last updated:** May 4, 2025

This privacy policy applies to all Chrome extensions developed and published by **Umesh Ghimire** on the Chrome Web Store.

---

### 🔒 1. Data Collection

We **do not collect**, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) or sensitive user data through any of our extensions.

All functionality operates locally within your browser. We do not access or transmit your browsing history, personal files, or any personal data.

---

### ⚙️ 2. Permissions and Usage

Some of our extensions may request browser permissions such as:
- Access to tabs
- Read/download access
- Content scripts

These permissions are strictly required for the extension's core functionality. **No data is ever sent to our servers or third parties.**

---

### 🧩 3. Third-Party Services

We do **not use** any third-party analytics, advertising networks, or tracking tools in our extensions.

---

### 🔐 4. Security and Local Operation

All code runs within the user's browser and processes data locally. We follow best practices to ensure that your experience remains private and secure.
